# Day 2 Design System

## Typography

- Display: used for high-visibility marketing statements and page hero moments.
- Heading: used for section titles and content hierarchy.
- Subheading: used for introductory summaries and business context.
- Body: used for descriptive content and forms.
- Caption: used for helper text, timestamps, and metadata.
- Label: used for filters, form labels, and status labels.
- Numeric: used for score values, KPIs, and metrics.

## Spacing

- Use an 8px base spacing scale to maintain rhythm and improve consistency.
- Pacing is determined by spacing tokens rather than ad hoc measurements.

## Semantic Colors

- Success: green for operationally healthy states.
- Warning: amber for watch and moderate concerns.
- Danger: red for critical or urgent actions.
- Info: blue for neutral updates and general information.
- Neutral: gray for surfaces, dividers, and secondary content.
- Critical/High/Medium/Low: severity tokens include text, icon, and label patterns, not only color.

## Surfaces and Radius

- Surfaces use layered elevation and subtle borders to create premium SaaS depth.
- Radius tokens are applied consistently to cards, pills, inputs, and action buttons.

## Motion

- Interaction timing stays subtle and fast.
- The product supports reduced-motion preferences and keeps transitions purposeful.

## Themes

- Light and dark themes share the same semantic design tokens.
- Theme is stored locally in the browser and defaults to the system preference when available.
